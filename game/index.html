HOW TO PLAY

MISSION OBJECTIVE

Command your elite team of librarians to neutralize sentient overdue books before they escape and spread chaos!

CONTROLS

  • LEFT CLICK: Select unit / Move / Attack
  • RIGHT CLICK: Cancel action / Deselect
  • 1-4 KEYS: Quick select abilities
  • SPACE: End turn
  • TAB: Cycle through units
  • ESC: Open menu

COMBAT BASICS

  • Each unit has Action Points (AP) per turn
  • Moving costs 1 AP per tile
  • Attacking costs 2 AP
  • Use COVER (bookshelves) to reduce incoming damage
  • Set OVERWATCH to react to enemy movement
  • Different books have different weaknesses!

YOUR TEAM

  • HEAD LIBRARIAN: Balanced fighter, can rally allies
  • ARCHIVIST: Long range, high accuracy
  • CATALOGUER: Fast, can mark enemies
  • CONSERVATOR: Healer, can repair morale

THE ENEMY

  • ROMANCE NOVELS: Can charm and confuse
  • HORROR BOOKS: Cause fear, high damage
  • TEXTBOOKS: Tough, slow, heavy hitters
  • COMIC BOOKS: Fast, dodge attacks
  • ENCYCLOPEDIAS: Boss enemies, very dangerous
